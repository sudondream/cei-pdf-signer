First Launch

Prima Lansare

macOS will refuse to open it the first time, saying it cannot verify the app is free of malware. This is expected: the app is not signed with a paid Apple certificate. Open it anyway: double-click the app, press Done (never "Move to Trash"), then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click Open Anyway next to the app's name. Confirm, and enter an administrator password if asked. Every launch after that is normal.

La prima deschidere macOS va refuza aplicatia, spunand ca nu poate verifica daca este lipsita de programe daunatoare. Este normal: aplicatia nu este semnata cu un certificat Apple platit. Deschide-o oricum: dublu-click pe aplicatie, apasa Done (niciodata "Move to Trash"), apoi mergi in System Settings → Privacy & Security, deruleaza pana la sectiunea Security si apasa Open Anyway in dreptul aplicatiei. Confirma si introdu o parola de administrator daca ti se cere. Urmatoarele lansari sunt normale.

On older macOS versions the shortcut was to right-click the app and choose "Open". That no longer works.

Pe versiuni mai vechi de macOS se folosea click dreapta pe aplicatie si "Open". Aceasta metoda nu mai functioneaza.
